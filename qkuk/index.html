> Winrate watchlist accuracy

every reclaim scored mechanically — entries you never took

Every watchlist row is scored by a fixed rule: entry at the reclaim close, stop at the sweep extreme, symmetric 1R target, whichever is hit first within 7 days. If one bar touches both, it counts as a loss; timeouts are scored from the last close, never discarded. This measures raw entry quality across every detection — your own filtering on top is exactly what the raw number cannot see.
