Three channels, judged separately

The same detection logic runs on three timeframes. Each keeps its own log, its own de-duplication and its own scoreboard, so one cannot flatter another. The 1-hour channel is an evaluation channel: it publishes its watchlist but its entry signals are recorded only, never sent.

How a rule earns its place

Most ideas tested here are rejected. That is the point of the process, and the reason the numbers above can be published without editing.

01

Pre-registration

The decision rule and its thresholds are written down before the data is cut. A finding that only becomes interesting after the result is seen does not count.

02

Deflated Sharpe, PBO, CPCV

Every candidate is charged for the number of trials it took to find it. A rule that passes only because it was the best of six thousand is not a rule.

03

Shadow before live

New channels run silently against real market data first, scored on the same gates as the live ones. The 1-hour channel is in this stage now.

04

Decay alarm

Live performance is compared against the backtest reference on a rolling window, with the alarm thresholds fixed in advance. It reports; it never silently intervenes.
