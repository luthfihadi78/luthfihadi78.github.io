  • Decides on its own. Signal engines in Python read live futures data on two timeframes; entry, stop and target are fixed before a position opens.
  • Has to earn production. Deflated Sharpe, PBO and purged cross-validation gate every strategy. Most are rejected — that is the point of the gate.

Research · LIPI Bandung · 2016 — 2018

WoodID

Automated wood species identification · desktop & mobile

0species
100%accuracy on 6 of 12
0copyrights

Identifies a wood's species from an image of its surface, using Local Binary Pattern and Hough Transform. I built version one in C as a desktop application and developed the interface, so forestry staff could operate it rather than only the people who wrote it. Validated against thousands of specimens with the Ministry of Forestry and presented at the Forestry postgraduate programme at IPB.

The comparison mattered: LBP proved the stronger method, identifying six of the twelve species with perfect accuracy. Published at ICODIS 2018 through IOP Science, and registered as two Indonesian software copyrights — WoodID itself and LignoIndo, the commercial wood image database behind it.
