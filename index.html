How it works

From raw candles to a message

Five stages. The interesting one is the third — most ideas die there, and that's the point.

01

Collect

Live price and volume, pulled continuously and cached against rate limits.

02

Detect

Engines scan every pair for the market structure they were designed to find.

03

Validate

Tested on years of history, out-of-sample. Most ideas are rejected here.

04

Deliver

What survives goes to the group and the dashboard, with its levels attached.

05

Monitor

An alarm watches for decay and says when an edge has stopped working.
